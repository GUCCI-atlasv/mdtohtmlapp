Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

The short version

Your documents and exported page content are processed locally in your browser. mdtohtml has no accounts, no paid plan and no database of your documents or exported pages. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

The mdtohtml website

Markdown conversion, theming and preview run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type, paste, upload or import is sent to our servers. When you create an instant share link, your document is compressed into the part of the URL after the # symbol (the fragment). Browsers do not send URL fragments to servers, so shared documents are decoded and rendered only on the reader's device.

Save Page as HTML browser extension

When you invoke the extension, it temporarily accesses the active browser tab to read the page content needed for the feature you requested. Depending on the selected export scope, this may include the current page's text, images, links, code blocks, title and URL. The extension uses this information only to create the HTML or Markdown file, copy Markdown at your request, let you select a page region, and optionally include source information in the exported file.

Page content is processed locally in your browser. The extension does not upload exported page content to mdtohtml or third-party servers, does not access pages in the background, and does not build or store a browsing history. Generated files are saved to your device through the browser's download feature.

Local storage

The website may store your current draft and appearance preference in browser storage so you do not lose your work. The extension stores only local export preferences, such as the save scope, source-information setting and content-cleaning setting. Exported page content and browsing history are not stored in extension storage. Local data stays on your device and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Feedback and support email

The extension's feedback form prepares an email to support@mdtohtml.app in your system's default email application. You review and confirm the email before it is sent. If you choose to send feedback, the email may contain your email address, feedback message, extension version and, only when you leave the option enabled, the relevant page title and URL. We use this information only to respond to support requests and improve the product. The extension does not submit feedback automatically or upload it in the background.

Website operations

Our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived server logs (IP address, user agent, requested page) for security and abuse prevention. We may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to count page views in aggregate. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell data to anyone.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and JavaScript libraries from the cdnjs content delivery network. These providers may receive standard web requests (such as your IP address) when your browser fetches those files. No document content is included in these requests.

No accounts, no payments

The Service has no accounts and no billing. We do not create user profiles or collect payment information. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily include in that message solely so we can handle your request.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use data accessed through extension permissions only to provide or improve the extension's user-facing, single-purpose functionality. We do not sell or transfer this data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending or purposes unrelated to that functionality.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@mdtohtml.app or use the feedback form.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

The short version

Your documents never leave your browser. mdtohtml is free, has no accounts, no paid plan, no database of your content, and no interest in your data.

Your documents

Markdown conversion, theming and preview run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type, paste, upload or import is sent to our servers. When you create an instant share link, your document is compressed into the part of the URL after the # symbol (the fragment). Browsers do not send URL fragments to servers, so shared documents are decoded and rendered only on the reader's device.

Local storage

To improve your experience, we store two things in your browser's local storage: your current draft (so you don't lose work when closing the tab) and your dark-mode preference. This data stays on your device and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

What we do collect

Our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived server logs (IP address, user agent, requested page) for security and abuse prevention. We may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to count page views in aggregate. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell data to anyone.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and JavaScript libraries from the cdnjs content delivery network. These providers may receive standard web requests (such as your IP address) when your browser fetches those files. No document content is included in these requests.

No accounts, no payments

The Service is free forever and has no Pro tier, no accounts and no billing. We hold no user profiles, no email addresses and no payment information. There is nothing to log in to and nothing to delete.
